Tiny Foodie Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Tiny Foodie ("the app") is built local-first: there are no accounts, and your baby's profile - birth month, tried foods, allergen status, saved recipes - lives only on your device. You can delete all of it in one tap from Settings, and deleting the app deletes it too.

What leaves your phone

Menu photos and dish text. When you scan a menu or check a dish, the photo or text is sent to our serverless proxy, which forwards it to OpenAI for reading. It is used only to produce your verdicts and is not retained by us or used to identify you.

Anonymous usage events. The app sends basic, anonymous instrumentation (for example, that a scan completed) tagged with a random install identifier generated on first launch. It is not linked to you, your device, or your child.

Purchases. If you buy Tiny Foodie Plus, the purchase is processed by Apple and recorded by RevenueCat under an anonymous app user ID so your subscription works and can be restored.

What never leaves your phone

Your child's name, birth month, tried-foods and allergen profile, saved recipes, and verdict history are stored locally with SwiftData and are never transmitted.

Children's privacy

The app is used by parents, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the design goal is to know as little about your child as possible - everything personal stays on your device.

Third-party processors

OpenAI (menu and recipe reading), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Apple (App Store distribution and payments).

Your choices

Delete everything from Settings at any time. To manage or cancel a subscription, use your Apple ID subscription settings. Questions: yazbuilds@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
